What this number is

This number is a private, personal assistant used by one individual, its owner. The owner texts questions or requests to the number from their own mobile phone, and an automated assistant texts back the answer.

Who receives messages

Only the owner's own mobile phone. The number does not send marketing, promotions or messages of any kind to anyone else, and it never sends a first message on its own. Every conversation starts with the owner texting the number.

How opt-in works

The owner opts in by texting the number from their phone. Replies are sent only in response to those messages. No phone numbers are collected, bought or shared.

Opting out and help

  • Text STOP to stop all messages. Text START to resume.
  • Text HELP for help.
  • Message frequency varies with use. Message and data rates may apply.

Privacy

Messages are used only to answer the owner's requests. No message content or phone numbers are sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing. Mobile information is not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Terms
